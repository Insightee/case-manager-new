Clinical Summary

Manan presents as a highly inquisitive and observant 7-year-old child diagnosed with Autism Spectrum Disorder (Level 1). Over the observation period, Manan demonstrated exceptional cognitive abilities in specific domains, particularly pattern recognition and complex mechanical logic. While his expressive language is advanced, social-pragmatic communication remains a primary area for intervention. He exhibits notable sensory sensitivity to high-frequency environmental noise, which directly impacts his emotional regulation in classroom settings. The clinical findings suggest that with tailored visual supports and sensory-aware environments, Manan’s ability to navigate transitions and social interactions improves significantly.
