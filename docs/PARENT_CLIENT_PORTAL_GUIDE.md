# Client Portal Guide for Parents & Guardians

Welcome to **InsighteCase** — your family’s online home for therapy updates, scheduling, documents, and support. This guide explains what you can do in the portal and how to find your way around, step by step.

---

## What is the Client Portal?

The Client Portal is a secure website where you can:

- See **session updates** after your therapist’s visit notes are reviewed and shared with you  
- **Book, reschedule, or cancel** therapy sessions (when your care plan allows)  
- Review **IEP plans**, goals, and shared documents for each child  
- **Approve absences**, respond to new care assignments, and stay on top of notifications  
- Open **support tickets** or **report incidents** to your care team  
- View **case manager meetings** you are invited to  
- Keep your **profile and service address** up to date  

You only see information that your care team has **approved and shared** with families. Clinical notes that are still in review stay on the therapist and admin side until they are ready for you.

## Getting started

### 1. Activate your account (first time)

If Insighte invited you by email:

1. Open the **invite link** from your email.  
2. Enter your name and choose a **password** (at least 6 characters).  
3. Tap **Activate account**.  
4. You will land on your **Family dashboard**.

### 2. Sign in later

1. Go to the **Client sign-in** page (`/clientlogin`).  
2. Enter the **email** on your account and your **password**.  
3. Optional: turn on **Remember me** so you stay signed in on your device.  
4. Tap **Sign in**.

**Tip:** Use the **Client** login page — not the Therapist or Admin pages. If you use the wrong portal, the app will ask you to sign in on the correct one.

### 3. Forgot your password?

1. On the sign-in page, open **Forgot password**.  
2. Enter your email and follow the reset link sent to you.  
3. Set a new password on the reset page.

Your sign-in email is shown on **My profile** and can only be changed by contacting support.

## How navigation works

The portal is designed for **phones and computers**. The same features are available on both; only the layout changes.

### On a computer (wider screen)

A **sidebar on the left** lists all main areas:

| Menu item | What it is |
|-----------|------------|
| **Dashboard** | Your home page — summary of cases, next session, alerts |
| **Session updates** | All approved session logs across your children |
| **Session schedule** | Upcoming appointments + book / reschedule / cancel |
| **Reports** | Monthly reports, IEP, and documents *(see note below)* |
| **Billing** | Invoices and payments *(see note below)* |
| **My profile** | Your details, children, and service address |
| **Support & Incidents** | Help tickets and incident reports |
| **Meetings** | Case manager meetings you are part of |

At the bottom of the sidebar you can open **Notifications**, go to **My profile**, or **Sign out**.

### On a phone (mobile)

- **Bottom bar:** **Home** · **Sessions** · **Reports** · **Billing** — quick access to the most-used pages.  
- **☰ Menu (top left):** Opens the full menu (schedule, profile, support, meetings, and the rest).  
- **Bell icon (top):** Notifications.

## Your home: Family dashboard

After sign-in you arrive at **Dear [your name],** — the family dashboard.

Here you will typically see:

### Next session

Shows your **upcoming therapy session or case manager meeting**. Tap it for details, or use **Full schedule →** to open the session schedule page.

If nothing is booked, you will see a link to **Book a session**.

### Absence approvals

If the school or care team requested approval for a **child absence**, it appears here. Review and approve when prompted.

### Recent session updates

A short list of the latest **approved** session summaries. Tap any row to open that child’s case, or **View all →** for the full session updates feed.

### Action alerts (when something needs you)

Colored banners may appear for:

- **Invoices** awaiting payment or overdue *(when billing is enabled)*  
- **IEP reviews** waiting for your acknowledgement  

Tap the banner to go straight to the right place.

### New care assignment

When a therapist and schedule are assigned to a new case, a banner lets you:

- Tap **I have reviewed this assignment** (optional but helpful for the team)  
- Or **Report a problem** if something looks wrong

### Your active cases

Each card shows:

- **Child name** and **case reference**  
- **Service type** (e.g. homecare, shadow support)  
- **Therapist** and **case manager**  
- **Latest approved report** month  

Use **Latest report** or **Review IEP** when those actions are available.

### Recent notifications

Tap a notification to mark it as read. Use **Open notifications page →** for the full list.

### Summary tiles

Quick counts for **active cases**, **recent updates**, **IEP pending**, and **unread alerts** — each tile links to the relevant section.

## Session updates

**Menu:** Session updates · **Mobile:** Sessions

This page lists **approved session logs** — summaries of what happened in therapy, written for families.

### Filters

- **Child** — one child or all children  
- **Month** — browse the last 12 months  
- **Attendance** — completed, partial, no show, or cancelled  

Use **Schedule →** to book a session from here.

### What each session card shows

Typical sections (when your therapist filled them in):

- **Update for family** — main message from the therapist  
- **What we did today**  
- **Goals worked on**  
- **What’s next**  

You may also see **case manager meeting** cards with meeting notes (concerns, follow-up, actions).

### Your feedback

On each session you can:

- Give a **star rating** (1–5)  
- Add **optional comments**  
- Choose whether to **share feedback on the therapist’s public profile**  
- Tap **Save**  

If something looks incorrect, tap **Dispute** — this opens a **support ticket** with the session details prefilled.

## Session schedule

**Menu:** Session schedule

Manage **therapy appointments** (not case manager meetings — those are booked by your case manager).

### Upcoming sessions

Scroll the list of upcoming sessions. Tap one to open a detail sheet where you can:

- **Add to Google Calendar**  
- **Reschedule** (if allowed for that session)  
- **Cancel** (if allowed)  

Status badges:

- **Confirmed** — approved and on the calendar  
- **Pending approval** — waiting for therapist confirmation  
- **Cancelled**  

**Case manager meetings** in this list are **view only**. Contact your case manager to change them.

### Book a new session

1. Choose your **child / case**.  
2. Choose the **therapist** (if more than one is available).  
3. Pick a **date**.  
4. Select an **open time slot**.  
5. Confirm the booking.

After booking, you may see a success message and option to add the session to your calendar.

### Reschedule

From an upcoming session’s detail sheet, tap **Reschedule**, then pick a new date and slot. The form will guide you through the same steps as booking.

## Reports, IEP & documents

**Menu:** Reports · **Mobile:** Reports

> **Note:** The main **Reports** menu page may show **“Coming soon”** while billing and reports hub features roll out. You can still access many items from each **child’s case page** (see below).

When the full Reports hub is live, you will use it to:

- Read **monthly reports** published for your family  
- Review and **acknowledge IEP** documents  
- Open **shared documents** (PDFs and files from your care team)  
- Filter by child, report type, and status  

### IEP acknowledgement (important)

When an IEP is ready for you:

1. Open the child’s **case** (from the dashboard or profile).  
2. Go to the **IEP** tab.  
3. Open the document and read it.  
4. Tap **Acknowledge IEP** when you have reviewed it.  

The dashboard may also show an alert: **“IEP acknowledgement required.”**

## Child case page (your case hub)

Tap a **child’s name** on the dashboard or **View case →** on My profile.

Each case has **tabs**:

| Tab | What you can do |
|-----|-----------------|
| **Profile** | Case overview — therapist, case manager, service type, IEP status, quick links to reports |
| **Session updates** | Session logs for this child only |
| **Observation** | Observation reports shared with you |
| **IEP** | IEP documents — open, download, acknowledge |
| **Goals** | Goals mentioned in approved sessions over time |
| **Documents** | Files your team shared (open / download) |
| **Bookings** | Upcoming sessions for this case — cancel when allowed |

Use the tab bar to move between sections without leaving the case.

## My profile

**Menu:** My profile · **Mobile:** ☰ Menu → My profile

Keep your family’s details accurate so visits and documents go to the right place.

### Account

- **Full name** and **phone** — edit and save  
- **Login email** — read-only; contact support to change  

### Additional contact

Optional second person (co-parent, spouse) for appointments or updates — name and email.

### Children

- See each child’s name and linked **case codes**  
- **Edit** a child’s name (pencil icon) if spelling needs correction  
- **Add another child** if your family grows  
- **View case →** jumps to that child’s case hub  

### Service address

- Enter **Home** or **School** address (kept separate)  
- Pin location on the map when prompted (helps home visits)  
- Option: **Use this address as billing address**  
- Or enter a separate **billing address**  

Tap **Save profile** when you finish editing.

## Support & incidents

**Menu:** Support & Incidents · **Mobile:** ☰ Menu

Two tabs:

### Support tickets

For everyday questions and issues:

1. Choose a **topic** (billing, scheduling, therapist, case issue, etc.) so the right team receives it.  
2. Select the **case / child** if relevant.  
3. Write a **subject** and **message**.  
4. Attach files if needed.  
5. Submit — you will see your ticket in the list.

On an open ticket you can:

- **Reply** with more information  
- **Escalate** if you need faster attention  
- **Rate and close** when resolved (1–5 stars)  
- **Reopen** if the issue is not fixed  

A **policies helper** may be available for common questions.

**Dispute** on a session update also creates a support ticket with details prefilled.

### Incident reports

For serious safety or care incidents:

- View **open** and **closed** incidents you have filed  
- Tap **Report incident** to submit a new report (case, description, priority, attachments)  
- Expand an incident to see status and updates from the team

## Meetings

**Menu:** Meetings · **Mobile:** ☰ Menu

View **case manager meetings** where you are an attendee:

- Meeting type (IEP review, parent meeting, progress review, etc.)  
- Date, time, and duration  
- **Join meeting** link (when provided)  
- Notes after the meeting (concerns, follow-up, actions)  
- **Add to Google Calendar** for scheduled meetings  

Parents **do not book** these meetings in the portal — your case manager schedules them. Therapy sessions are booked under **Session schedule**.

## Notifications

Reach notifications from:

- The **bell** in the top bar (mobile) or sidebar footer  
- **Unread alerts** on the dashboard  
- **Open notifications page** from the dashboard panel  

Tap a notification to mark it read. The full page lists everything in one place.

## Signing out

- **Desktop:** **Sign out** at the bottom of the left sidebar.  
- **Mobile:** ☰ Menu → **Sign out** at the bottom of the drawer.

Sign out on shared devices (school kiosk, family tablet) to protect your child’s information.

## What you will *not* see (and why)

| You might expect… | In the portal… |
|-------------------|----------------|
| Therapist’s private clinical notes | Hidden until reviewed and approved for families |
| Draft reports | Only **published** monthly reports appear |
| Other families’ data | Your login only shows **your** cases and children |
| Instant session logs | Updates appear after therapist submit + team approval |

If the dashboard looks quiet right after a session, the log may still be going through review — check back later or ask via Support.

## Tips for a smooth experience

1. **Use Chrome or Safari** on your phone for the best layout; add the site to your home screen for one-tap access.  
2. **Keep your profile updated** — especially service address for home visits.  
3. **Watch your email** — the portal complements messages from your care team when something needs you.  
4. **One case per child** — if you have multiple children, each has their own case card and tabs.  
5. **Session feedback helps** — ratings and comments support your therapist and case manager.  
6. **Need help?** Support tickets are the fastest path; include your **case reference** and child’s name.
