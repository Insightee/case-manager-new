# Therapist Portal Guide

Welcome to **InsighteCase** — your workspace for sessions, case notes, reports, scheduling, and care coordination. This guide explains what you can do in the portal and how to find your way around, step by step.

---

## What is the Therapist Portal?

The Therapist Portal is a secure website where you can:

- Run **today’s sessions** and submit **session logs** after each visit  
- Work from **My Cases** — client history, observations, goals, and documents  
- Draft and submit **monthly reports** for admin review  
- Generate and track **invoices** for your completed work  
- Set **availability**, request **leave**, and join **case manager meetings**  
- Open **support tickets**, **report incidents**, and read team **memos**  
- Keep your **profile** and service details up to date  

Families see session updates only after you submit a log and your care team approves it for sharing. Until then, your clinical notes stay on the therapist and admin side.

## Getting started

### 1. Activate your account (first time)

If Insighte invited you by email:

1. Open the **invite link** from your email.  
2. Enter your name and choose a **password** (at least 6 characters).  
3. Tap **Activate account**.  
4. You will land on your **Therapist dashboard**.

### 2. Sign in later

1. Go to the **Therapist sign-in** page (`/therapistlogin`).  
2. Enter the **email** on your account and your **password**.  
3. Optional: turn on **Remember me** so you stay signed in on your device.  
4. Tap **Sign in**.

**Tip:** Use the **Therapist** login page — not the Client or Admin pages. If you use the wrong portal, the app will ask you to sign in on the correct one.

### 3. Forgot your password?

1. On the sign-in page, open **Forgot password**.  
2. Enter your email and follow the reset link sent to you.  
3. Set a new password on the reset page.

Your sign-in email is shown on **My profile** and can only be changed by contacting support.

## How navigation works

The portal is designed for **phones and computers**. The same features are available on both; only the layout changes.

### On a computer (wider screen)

A **sidebar on the left** lists all main areas:

| Menu item | What it is |
|-----------|------------|
| **Dashboard** | Your home page — today’s schedule, logs due, quick actions |
| **My Cases** | All assigned children and case workbenches |
| **Session Logs** | Start visits, submit logs, review status |
| **Monthly Reports** | Draft, submit, and track monthly case reports |
| **Invoices** | Generate and track your invoices |
| **Support & Incidents** | Support tickets, incident reports, memos |
| **Meetings** | Case manager meetings you are part of |
| **Leave** | Request and track leave |
| **Scheduling** | Set weekly availability and open slots |

Open **My profile** from the sidebar footer. At the bottom you can also reach **Notifications** or **Sign out**.

### On a phone (mobile)

- **Bottom bar:** **Today** · **Cases** · **Reports** · **Home** — quick access to the most-used pages.  
- **☰ Menu (top left):** Opens the full menu (logs, invoices, support, leave, scheduling, profile, and the rest).  
- **Bell icon (top):** Notifications.

## Your home: Therapist dashboard

After sign-in you arrive at your **Therapist dashboard** — a greeting with your first name and what needs you next.

The dashboard has two views:

- **Overview** — today’s work, stats, and pending actions  
- **Therapist Updates** — support and operational updates in one place  

Here you will typically see:

### Work summary tiles

Quick counts linking to the right place:

- **Assigned cases**  
- **Sessions need log** — visits completed but not yet logged  
- **Logs pending approval** — submitted and waiting for review  
- **Report drafts** — monthly reports still in progress  

### Pending actions

A prioritized list when something needs you, such as:

- **Session log due** — tap to open the log for that visit  
- **CM meeting notes** — follow-up after a case manager meeting  

### Today’s schedule

Upcoming visits for today. Tap a row to open **Session logs** or the client’s **case**. Use **Open session logs →** for the full logs workspace.

### New case assignment

When you are assigned a new case, a banner appears. You can **start sessions and logs right away**. Optionally tap **Mark as reviewed** after you have read the care plan. If the family has not yet accepted the assignment, you may see **Waiting for parent** — you can still log sessions.

### Cases needing attention

Cases flagged for overdue reports, missing logs, or other follow-ups. Tap a name to open the case, or **View all cases →** for the full filtered list.

### Quick actions

Shortcut cards for frequent tasks — for example **Submit daily log**, **My cases**, **Monthly reports**, **Invoices**, **Request leave**, and **Scheduling**. The portal learns which you use most and surfaces those first.

## My Cases

**Menu:** My Cases · **Mobile:** Cases

Your case workbench for every child assigned to you.

### What you see

- **Stats** — active cases, attention needed, due soon  
- **Upcoming sessions** — next visits across your caseload  
- **Case cards** grouped by stage — for example active, needs attention, onboarding  

Each card shows the **child name**, **case reference**, **service type**, next due item, and quick links.

### Filters and search

- Search by child name or case code  
- Filter by **stage**, **service type**, or **due soon**  
- Sort by urgency or name  
- Switch between **grid** and **table** view  

Tap a case card or **View case →** to open that child’s **case hub**.

## Case page (your case hub)

Each assigned case has **tabs**:

| Tab | What you can do |
|-----|-----------------|
| **Overview** | Client history, operational timeline, case manager contact, clinical snapshot (strengths, goals, and related context) |
| **Observation** | Observation checklist and reports for the case |
| **Sessions & logs** | Scheduled visits and session history for this child only |
| **Documents** | Files shared on the case — open or download |

### Request a status change

From the case header, use **Request change** if the case should be suspended, closed, or reactivated. This sends a request for admin approval — the case stays as-is until reviewed.

If a status request is already pending, a banner appears under the header.

### Case manager

The **Case manager** card shows who coordinates this case — name, email, and a link to reach support if needed.

## Session logs

**Menu:** Session Logs · **Mobile:** Today

This is where you **start visits**, **submit daily logs**, and track approval status. Aim to finish a log soon after each session while details are fresh.

### Starting a session

At the top of Session logs you can:

1. **Start today’s session** — pick a scheduled visit or a walk-in for today  
2. **Log a past session** — if you forgot to log on the visit day (within allowed rules)  
3. **Log child absence** — when the child was unavailable for a scheduled visit  

**Visit-day rule:** You can only **start** a scheduled session on its **scheduled date** (today in IST). Future visits show guidance instead of a start button.

**Walk-in today:** For an unscheduled visit today, choose the client, session mode (Home, School, Center, Online), and actual start/end times.

**Second session same day:** If you already logged a session for the same child today, the portal asks **Another session today?** You can open the existing log or confirm starting another — the second log may be flagged for review.

### While a session is active

An **Active session** card appears with the visit in progress. Open it to continue logging or end the session when you finish.

### Submitting the log

After the visit, fill in structured sections — for example what you worked on, goals, strategies, progress signals, and the **update for family** (the message parents may see after approval).

Tap **Save draft** to continue later, or **Submit** when ready. Submitted logs move to **Pending review** until your case manager or admin approves them.

### Log tabs and filters

| Tab | What it shows |
|-----|----------------|
| **Needs log** | Visits that happened but still need a submitted log *(not filtered by month)* |
| **All logs** | Everything for the selected month |
| **Child absent** | Absence records you filed |
| **Leave** | Leave-related log entries |
| **Pending review** | Submitted, awaiting approval |
| **Approved** | Approved logs *(families may see approved family-facing sections)* |
| **Rejected** | Sent back — open, revise, and resubmit |

Use the **month and year** filter for historical tabs. **Needs log** always shows all open items regardless of month.

### Edit actual times

After you submit a log, you can **Edit times** within **24 hours** of the session end if the clock times were wrong. Add a short reason when saving. The log returns to **Pending review** and may show a **Times edited** badge.

### Rejected logs

Open a rejected log, read the reviewer’s comments, update your entries, and **Resubmit**.

## Monthly reports

**Menu:** Monthly Reports · **Mobile:** Reports

End-of-month summaries built from your session logs and case context.

### Report pipeline

Reports move through stages such as:

- **Draft** — still editing  
- **Under review** — submitted to admin / case manager  
- **Published** — approved and available to the care team (and families when shared)  
- **Overdue** — past the due window for that month  

The page shows **attention needed**, **in progress**, and **published** sections, plus a monthly checklist to keep you on track.

### Creating and editing a report

1. Open **Monthly Reports**.  
2. Start a **new draft** for a case and month, or open an existing draft.  
3. Review session activity for that month — you can generate draft content from logs where available.  
4. Edit sections, save, then **Submit for review**.  
5. If a report is **rejected**, open it, address feedback, and resubmit.

Use **case filters** and search when you manage many reports at once.

## Invoices

**Menu:** Invoices

Generate and track payment for your completed sessions.

### Typical workflow

1. Review your **session activity** for the billing period.  
2. **Generate an invoice** for the chosen month (the form shows what will be included).  
3. Preview the breakdown before submitting.  
4. Track status — draft, submitted, approved, paid — from the invoice list.  

Use the earnings summary and checklist on the page to stay current. For payout questions, open a **Support ticket** with a finance topic.

## Support & incidents

**Menu:** Support & Incidents · **Mobile:** ☰ Menu

Three tabs:

### Support tickets

For everyday questions and operational issues:

1. Choose a **request type** (HR, finance, service, scheduling, case issue, and more) so the right team receives it.  
2. Select the **case / child** if relevant.  
3. Write a **subject** and **message**.  
4. Attach files if needed.  
5. Submit — your ticket appears in the list.

On an open ticket you can **reply**, follow status updates, and close when resolved. A **policies helper** may be available for common HR or finance questions.

You can also reach support from a case page via the support shortcut.

### Incident reports

For serious safety or care incidents:

- View **open** and **closed** incidents you have filed  
- Tap **Report incident** to submit a new report (case, description, priority, attachments)  
- Expand an incident to see status and updates from the team  

### Memos received

Internal memos and announcements sent to therapists — read and acknowledge when prompted.

## Meetings

**Menu:** Meetings · **Mobile:** ☰ Menu

View **case manager meetings** where you are an attendee:

- Meeting type (observation review, IEP meeting, progress review, parent meeting, and more)  
- Date, time, and duration  
- **Join meeting** link when provided  
- Notes after the meeting (concerns, follow-up, actions)  
- **Add to Google Calendar** for scheduled meetings  

Therapists may **book** certain meeting types for their cases when the portal allows it. Parent-bookable therapy sessions are managed separately under **Scheduling** and parent booking — not here.

After a meeting, complete any **CM meeting notes** tasks linked from your dashboard.

## Leave

**Menu:** Leave · **Mobile:** ☰ Menu

Request time away and see how it affects your caseload.

### Request leave

1. Tap **Request leave** (or open the form from a quick action).  
2. Choose **dates** and leave **category** (paid, carry forward, unpaid, and other options your organization enables).  
3. Select affected **cases** when prompted.  
4. Add a **reason** and confirm whether you consulted with parents if required.  
5. Submit — track status as **Pending**, **Approved**, or **Rejected**.

### Leave balance

Your **balance summary** and calendar view show approved leave and remaining credits when your organization maintains balances.

When you are on approved leave, related sessions may show as **therapist unavailable** for families.

## Scheduling

**Menu:** Scheduling · **Mobile:** ☰ Menu

Mark when you are **available** for sessions so parents and coordinators can book open slots.

### Weekly calendar

- Tap an **empty cell** to add an availability slot  
- Tap an existing **slot** to view, edit, or remove it  
- Tap a **booked session** to jump to that case’s sessions tab  

### Weekly schedule and recurring slots

Use **Weekly schedule** to set your regular availability pattern, or **Book recurring** for repeating open slots across weeks.

Keep availability updated when your routine changes — especially before families book new sessions.

## My profile

**Menu:** My profile · **Mobile:** ☰ Menu → profile (via drawer footer or account area)

Keep your details accurate for coordination, visits, and HR records.

### Account

- **Full name** and **phone** — edit and save  
- **Login email** — read-only; contact support to change  
- **Employment status** — for example Active or Suspended *(Suspended may limit new bookings)*  
- **Profile photo** — upload or update your avatar  

### Home address

Enter your home location (address and map pin when prompted). This helps routing and service planning where your organization uses it.

### Service profile

Your **service profile** — specialties, modes, and related service metadata — may be visible to coordinators. Update when your scope changes.

### Reviews

Families can leave **ratings and feedback** on approved sessions. Your profile may show aggregated reviews when shared publicly.

Tap **Save** when you finish editing.

## What families see (and what stays with the care team)

| You enter… | Families typically see… |
|------------|-------------------------|
| Full session log with clinical detail | Only **approved**, family-facing sections (for example “Update for family”) |
| Draft or pending log | Nothing yet — still in review |
| Rejected log you are revising | Previous approved update until a new version is approved |
| Monthly report draft | Nothing until published and shared |
| Private case notes and documents | Only files and reports explicitly shared with families |

If a parent says they did not see today’s update, the log may still be **pending review** — check the log status or follow up with your case manager.

## Tips for a smooth experience

1. **Log sessions the same day** whenever possible — **Needs log** stays manageable and families get updates sooner after approval.  
2. **Use a phone in the field** — primary actions sit in thumb-friendly areas; add the site to your home screen for one-tap access.  
3. **Structured fields first** — complete goals, strategies, and progress signals; use free text to enrich, not replace, structured entries.  
4. **Watch pending review** — rejected or flagged logs (same-day duplicate, edited times) need a quick follow-up.  
5. **Keep scheduling current** — outdated availability causes booking conflicts and support tickets.  
6. **Include the case reference** in support messages so the team can help faster.  
7. **Use Chrome or Safari** for the best mobile layout.

## Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Case** | A child’s active care enrollment with Insighte — your hub for sessions, reports, and documents |
| **Case manager** | Your main coordinator at Insighte for reviews, meetings, and escalations |
| **Session log** | Your record of a therapy visit — may include family-facing and clinical sections |
| **Needs log** | A completed visit waiting for you to submit the log |
| **Pending review** | Submitted log or report awaiting case manager or admin approval |
| **CM meeting** | Meeting led or coordinated by the case manager (distinct from a therapy session) |
| **Walk-in** | A session you started today without a pre-booked slot |
| **Availability slot** | A time you marked open for booking on the scheduling calendar |
